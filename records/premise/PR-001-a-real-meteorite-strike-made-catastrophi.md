A genuine micrometeorite strike hits Station Aurora. Damage spreads abnormally because the replacement engineer, Alistair Johnson, secretly disabled the station's automatic impact-isolation safeguards beforehand. The strike is real; Alistair did not summon it. This keeps the accident and the conspiracy in one story.
