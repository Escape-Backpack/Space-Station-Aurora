## How it works
Merge gate: life support L12, power P806, medical M-26 and sponsor EV-2702 must all be recorded. HAROLD reports the D3-C3 corridor sealed. Using only solid walking corridors and the transparent route overlay, players go from Crew Quarters D3 to Evacuation Dock B5, reading each room's hatch digit (skip the start, include the destination). Solid corridors: D3-C3, C3-C4, D3-D4, D4-C4, C4-B4, B4-B5.

## Player obtains
8536. HAROLD: navigation restored, Console Phase B authorized.

## Solution
D3 -> D4 -> C4 -> B4 -> B5, digits 8, 5, 3, 6.

## Hints
1. Block D3-C3; use solid corridors.
2. Begin D3 to D4 and do not revisit a room.
3. D4, C4, B4, B5 gives 8536.
