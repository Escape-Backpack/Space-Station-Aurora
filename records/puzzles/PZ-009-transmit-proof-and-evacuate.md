## How it works
HAROLD shows an emergency protocol card (01 impact, 02 fire, 03 deliberate sabotage). Players select 03 and complete a structured report cross-matching the evidence: responsible engineer, sponsor, payment reference, work order, credential, disabled system and relative timing. Short normalized inputs for IDs, choices for system and timing; wrong answers name the field to recheck without wiping the report. All five system states must be complete before TRANSMIT EVIDENCE appears; then INITIATE EVACUATION.
