## How it works
Three cartridges (A capacity 4 / rack load 2 / back label L; B 6 / 4 / X; C 8 / 3 / 12) and a two-slot cradle. Cradle label: install exactly TWO cartridges, required capacity exactly 12, maximum combined rack load 5, lower capacity in the LEFT slot, read the back labels left to right.

## Player obtains
The acknowledgement **L12**, reported to HAROLD, which then shows life support stable. These are fictional scrubber units.

## Solution
Only A+C works (4+8=12, load 2+3=5). Other pairs give capacity 10 or 14. Smaller first gives L12. All three blocks must fit either slot so fit does not reveal the answer.

## Hints
1. Both totals matter.
2. Find a pair totalling 12.
3. A+C, smaller first, gives L12.
