## How it works
The crew profiles list Pascale Tremblay (original engineer) as allergic to peanuts and shellfish and absent. The Fish and Nuts receipt (24 Feb 2032, customer A. Johnson) lists peanuts, walnuts and shellfish product. The private mnemonic on pouch E reads: "The shopping that got me aboard. Whole receipt total, four digits. Ignore the decimal."

## Player obtains
Code 2912 opens the PERSONAL RECORDS pouch (E): medical follow-up M-26, replacement order R-25, route overlay and evacuation procedure. HAROLD asks "Who was replaced?" (Pascale Tremblay) then for the follow-up record ID (M-26).

## Solution
Receipt total 29.12 -> 2912.

## Hints
1. Compare the absent engineer's allergies with the receipt.
2. Use the whole total, not just two ingredients.
3. 29.12 becomes 2912; open E and report M-26.

## Notes
The corrected receipt: peanuts 2.00, walnuts 2.63, shellfish product 17.50, packing service 3.53, subtotal 25.66, tax 13.5% 3.46, total 29.12. The old $6.99 tax line was arithmetically wrong. Purchases are suspicious, not proof.
