Alistair Johnson's rugged orange maintenance backpack. The outer bag stays open so players can see their goals. Contains locked pouches U and E, the Arduino box and the closed service panel.

## Player sees
An orange maintenance backpack with two labelled locks.

## Starting state
Pouches U and E locked; console drawers closed; service panel closed.

## Reset
Relock both pouches, close and lock both drawers, refasten the panel, return every prop to its container.

## Replacement
Not yet decided.
